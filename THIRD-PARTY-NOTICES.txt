Native libraries are dynamically loaded. To use a modified compatible
x64 LibVLC distribution, place libvlc.dll, libvlccore.dll and its plugins
in a libvlc directory beside LightMediaPlayer.exe.
Bundled license texts are extracted with the engine under:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\LightMediaPlayer\runtime\<payload hash>\licenses
This POC includes its application source and reproducible build scripts.
